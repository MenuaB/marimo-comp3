   enumeration, then make the explicit cut to a separate measured collection.
2. Choose a real training seed and show its nine-endpoint evidence strip.
3. Choose one cached ChemLlama proposal; retain its ID and call its experiments
   unknown.
4. Commit the fixed prediction-only fifty.
5. Reveal those same IDs: 41/50 meet the illustrative profile versus 20.99
   expected target passes under equal-size random selection.
6. Reveal Caco-2 for exactly those fifty: 38 have paired numeric values and 33
   of the 41 initial passes do.
7. Inspect a molecule and its nearest training context and selection stability.
8. Return to the original proposal and choose the next assay question.
